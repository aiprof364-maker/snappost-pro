# Facebook Page creation onboarding source

Date checked: 26 October 2026

The immediate SnapPost Pro onboarding email links customers without a business Facebook Page to Meta's official Page-creation guidance and makes the ownership boundary clear:

- The customer creates and manages the actual Facebook Page in Facebook.
- Manus or another AI may help them prepare a Page name, short description, and profile or cover-image ideas.
- The customer remains in control of Page access and management.

Official sources:

- Meta for Business, [How to create a Facebook Page for your business](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/473994396650734)
- Facebook Help Centre, [About Facebook Page access](https://www.facebook.com/help/289207354498410)
